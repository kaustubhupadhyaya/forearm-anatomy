Video Verification Two source videos tested

Video 1: four lifts cover 87.6%

Reverse curls, finger-roll wrist flexions, strap pronation and rising load 384.5 of 438.7 cm3 of contractile volume. The uncovered 12.4% is supinator, ECU and isolated terminal extensor work, which is exactly where the elbow pain in that video came from.

Video 2: rice bucket covers 85.1%

Twenty 30-second drills load 373.3 cm3 across flexion, extension, pronation, supination and both deviations. The miss is brachioradialis at 65.4 cm3: an elbow flexor with no wrist moment arm, so a stationary elbow gives it nothing.

Combined prescription

Heavy reverse curls and finger-roll wrist curls for upper-forearm mass; multi-directional rice work for antagonist balance, retinacular thickening and tenosynovial health. Neither alone covers the whole forearm.
